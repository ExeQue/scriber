# Review comments

Pin comments on the canvas, like review comments on a document. This is the easiest way to tell an
[AI agent](../agents/working-with-agents) what to change: it reads your comments, does the work,
replies and resolves them. Agents can leave notes for you the same way.

## Add a comment

- Annotate → **Comment**, then click a spot or a shape, type, and press **Comment** (or
  <kbd>Ctrl+Enter</kbd>).
- Or select shapes, right-click → **Comment on this…**.

## Pins and threads

- Each comment is a numbered speech bubble: open ones in the accent colour, resolved ones grey.
- Hover a pin to read it and its last reply; click it to open the thread: reply, **Resolve** or
  **Reopen**, **Delete**.
- The right panel lists all comments (*Comments, n open*). Click one to zoom to it.
- **Show comments** (Annotate or View tab) hides or shows the pins.

Comments are saved in the `.scrbr` file. They are never lasered or exported, and undo does not touch
them: undoing a drawing change never takes back a comment or a reply.
